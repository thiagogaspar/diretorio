    uid=22_39 StaticText "The supergroup is one of rock music's most fascinating experiments. When established musicians from different successful bands come together, the result can be either transcendent or disappointing. But when it works — as with "
    uid=22_40 StaticText "Audioslave"
    uid=22_41 StaticText ", "
    uid=22_42 StaticText "Temple of the Dog"
    uid=22_43 StaticText ", and "
    uid=22_44 StaticText "Them Crooked Vultures"
    uid=22_45 StaticText " — the music takes on a dimension that none of the member's primary projects could achieve alone."
    uid=22_46 heading "The Audioslave Story" level="2"
    uid=22_47 StaticText ""We didn't plan to form a band. We just went into the studio to jam, and three days later we had an album's worth of material.""
    uid=22_48 StaticText "Tom Morello, on the formation of Audioslave"
    uid=22_49 StaticText "In 2001, after the hiatus of Rage Against the Machine, guitarist Tom Morello, bassist Tim Commerford, and drummer Brad Wilk found themselves without a frontman. Enter "
    uid=22_50 StaticText "Chris Cornell"
    uid=22_51 StaticText ", the legendary voice of Soundgarden, who had just departed from that band. The chemistry was immediate."
    uid=22_52 image "Audioslave performing in 2005" url="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/5/51/Audioslave_2005_%28cropped%29.jpg/800px-Audioslave_2005_%28cropped%29.jpg"
    uid=22_53 StaticText "Audioslave at the 2005 tour — Cornell, Morello, Commerford, and Wilk"
    uid=22_54 StaticText "The band's self-titled debut album sold over three million copies, driven by hits like "
    uid=22_55 StaticText "Cochise"
    uid=22_56 StaticText " and "
    uid=22_57 StaticText "Like a Stone"
    uid=22_58 StaticText ". The combination of Cornell's soaring vocals with Morello's innovative guitar work created a sound that was neither Rage nor Soundgarden — it was something entirely new."
    uid=22_59 heading "Temple of the Dog: A Tribute That Became a Classic" level="2"
    uid=22_60 StaticText "Perhaps the most poignant supergroup in rock history, "
    uid=22_61 StaticText "Temple of the Dog"
    uid=22_62 StaticText " was formed in 1990 as a tribute to the late Andrew Wood, frontman of Mother Love Bone. The project brought together members of Soundgarden and what would become Pearl Jam, creating a single album that stands as a monument to friendship and grief."
